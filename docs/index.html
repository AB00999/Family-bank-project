Privacy notice

This notice describes the public static Family Bank concept page. It is not a privacy policy for a real financial institution.

Information and this page

This static page has no account registration or sign-in form. It does not ask for or send usernames, passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, or financial details. The sample sign-in display is an illustration and does not accept input. The page does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

The hosting provider may process basic technical request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the website. Check the hosting provider's privacy information for its practices. If you choose the email link, your email app and email provider handle the message under their own policies.

Do not submit sensitive or real account information through this concept site. The separate local Flask learning demo is not covered by this notice and should only be used with made-up test information.
