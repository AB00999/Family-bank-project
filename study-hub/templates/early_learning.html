For the grown-up

Play, pause, and keep children safe

Read quiz prompts aloud. Let the child answer with a look, gesture, movement, sound, or words, then tap their choice together. The gentle response is for conversation: there is no score and nothing is saved. Stay within arm's reach, follow toy age labels, and keep small or detachable objects away. The computer, cyber safety, and AI lessons use pretend play only; a young child does not need a device or an online account.

Make it fit the child

Adapt by age and attention

Birth-11 months

Keep play brief. An adult holds the book or object, names what the baby notices, and follows the baby's cues.

12-23 months

Use one simple word or choice at a time. Looking, touching, sounds, and gestures all count.

24-47 months

Offer simple choices and let the child lead. Repeat, move, or finish early whenever they prefer.
